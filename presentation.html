Domain: Healthcare Accessibility for Seniors (55+) & Families in India Why this domain? Standard medical apps overwhelm older adults with complex jargon and small text. Medibloom simplifies care with plain words, audio guides, and effortless Indian city doctor booking.

KEY FEATURES
& UI SHOWCASE

01

INTUITIVE NAVIGATION & FILTERING

Segmented care for Seniors (55+) and Children with synchronized live family progress tracking, text-size adjustments, and one-click dark/light mode toggle.

02

ACCESSIBLE AUDIO & 3-STATE TRACKING

Browser-native Web SpeechSynthesis reads prescriptions aloud; smart 3-state engine dynamically flags overdue doses with clear clinical safety instructions (do not take double dose).

03

SEAMLESS 3-STEP APPOINTMENT BOOKING

Frictionless booking wizard featuring top doctors across Indian cities (Bengaluru, Mumbai, Hyderabad, New Delhi) with real-time field validation and verified confirmation tickets.
